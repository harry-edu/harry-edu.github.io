APP NAME (max 30 characters)
Student Counselling Docket

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 characters)
Career and stream choices after Class 10 and 12, with self-scoring career tests

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4,000 characters)
Student Counselling Docket helps students in India, their parents and school counsellors choose subjects, courses and careers with the facts in hand. Developed by Dr. H. S. Khalsa.

AFTER CLASS 10
• Science with Maths, Science with Biology, Commerce and Humanities compared
• Careers for each stream, with leading institutions, entrance exams and outlook
• Diploma, ITI and skill routes directly after Class 10
• Traditional and new-age careers, and how artificial intelligence affects each
• Exam calendar, costs, scholarships, and myths and facts for parents

AFTER CLASS 12
• Courses and careers for PCM, PCB, Commerce and Humanities pass-outs
• Courses open to every stream: law, design, management, media, hospitality and more
• Cross-over and management careers: how a commerce student can work in health care, a humanities student in technology
• College directory by field, the admission calendar and how to apply to foreign universities
• Costs, scholarships, education loans and what to do if plans change

TWO CAREER INTEREST AND APTITUDE TESTS
• One test for Class 10 (best-fit stream) and one for Class 12 (best-fit career fields)
• Three parts: interests, reasoning (timed) and work style, plus career values
• Scores itself and links each result to the matching chapter of the docket
• The Class 12 test leaves out courses the student's Class 12 subjects do not allow
• Counsellors can type in answers from a paper booklet and save or share the report

FOR COUNSELLORS
• Fill-in counsellor's records, application trackers and action plans
• Search both dockets at once

Works offline. Everything you type stays on your phone: no sign-in, no ads, no data collected.

The tests are counselling aids, not standardised psychometric instruments. Exam dates, fees and rules change every year; confirm details on the official websites before applying.
