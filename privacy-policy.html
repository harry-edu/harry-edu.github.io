Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

What the app does

Student Counselling Docket is a reference and counselling app for students in India, their parents and school counsellors. It contains two counselling dockets (options after Class 10 and after Class 12) and two career interest and aptitude tests.

Information the app stores

When you use the app, it may store the following on your own device only:

  • test answers and results, and the student details typed into a test (such as name, class, school and stream);
  • entries typed into the counsellor's record and tracker tables;
  • ticked checklist items and the last section you read.

This information is kept in your device's app storage. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time with the app's "Start a new student" and "Clear this section" buttons, or by clearing the app's data in your phone settings.

Information the app does not collect

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and it does not share or sell any data.

Internet use

The app needs the internet only to load its typefaces and to open the official websites that it links to. Once opened, it works offline. Websites you open from the app have their own privacy policies.

Sharing a report

If you choose "Save or share the report", the app creates a report file on your device. Where it goes next is your choice, through your phone's own share or save screen.

Children

The app is meant for students in Class 10 and above, guided by a parent, teacher or counsellor. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
